About

Data-Driven Economist & Analytics Consultant

Grounded in economic theory, sharpened by data

I'm an economist with a background in applied statistics, specializing in time-series forecasting, experimental design, and macroeconomic analysis. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous methodology and practical business impact — I care as much about whether a model is statistically sound as whether it's actually useful to the people reading it.

Over the past several years I've worked with public datasets, survey microdata, and internal experimentation platforms to help teams answer questions like: is this policy working, is this price change moving demand, and where is the economy headed next quarter.

Tools of choice: Python and R for modeling, SQL for data wrangling, and a genuine belief that a well-labeled chart is worth more than a paragraph of caveats.
